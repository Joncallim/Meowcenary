## Final correction

The original Stage/Chapter section described the reusable boss-stage marker as an “industrial skull/ram-warning emblem”, while the later originality pass correctly rejected generic skull shorthand where Meowcenary has a stronger native visual vocabulary.

The final brief is therefore:

### Boss-stage marker — authoritative wording

**Compact compactor-jaw / toothed-gear hazard emblem** inside a clipped workshop warning frame. One heavy jaw/ram silhouette faces a smaller opposing plate, with two gear teeth or piston notches establishing industrial danger. Use cream/rust/danger-red accents but preserve the read in grayscale.

**Must not use:** human/animal skulls, generic fantasy boss crowns, military rank marks, copied warning logos or text.

This wording supersedes the skull option in `alpha-3-art-production-briefs.md` §14.
