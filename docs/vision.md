# Meowcenary Vision

## One-Line Pitch

A no-ads browser roguelite where a scrappy animal mercenary auto-fights enemy waves, merges improvised weapons, and grows stronger across short runs.

## Player Fantasy

The player is a tiny, overconfident survivor turning junk into absurd firepower. They should feel clever for building synergies, not punished for lacking aiming skill.

## Design References

- Gun Hero: approachability, cute combat, merge-driven weapon growth.
- Archero: movement-first combat, simple enemy readability, upgrade choices.
- Vampire Survivors: automatic attacks, escalating enemy pressure, build snowballing.

These references inform the product loop only. Visual identity, naming, UI layout, weapon art, enemy designs, balance values, and economy design must be original.

## Non-Negotiables

- No ads.
- No paid power upgrades.
- No energy systems.
- No mandatory accounts.
- No skill-shot aiming requirement.
- No IP copying from reference games.

## MVP Definition

A successful MVP lets a player complete a short run with:

- Smooth movement on keyboard and touch.
- Automatic firing at nearby enemies.
- Clear enemy waves.
- XP pickup and level-up choices.
- At least three weapon families.
- At least ten upgrade cards.
- Run win/loss states.
- Local save shell for meta progression.

## Success Criteria

- A first-time player understands the controls within 10 seconds.
- The first run becomes dangerous within 60 seconds.
- Each level-up choice has at least one obviously useful option.
- The player can feel a build getting stronger without needing a tutorial.
