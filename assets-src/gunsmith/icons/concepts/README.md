# Gunsmith icon-family concept provenance

Generated 2026-09-24 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The
selected Commando Equipment concept was supplied only as the established
industrial material/palette reference. These high-resolution boards are
retained production provenance, not runtime atlases.

## Shared brief

All three directions explore the exact current V4 presentation inventory:

- 12 physical Part identities from `gun-parts.json`;
- eight hollow slot glyphs for receiver, barrel, optic, stock, trigger,
  magazine, underbarrel, and trait;
- three flat behavior emblems for FIRE, EXPLOSIVE, and PIERCING.

Physical Parts must read as dimensional owned objects. Slot glyphs must read
as empty sockets. Trait emblems must remain structurally distinct from the
physical fire-core Parts. Every final identity targets 24–32 px presentation,
near-black outlines, clustered pixels, transparent negative space, and no
text, insignia, skulls, protected symbols, or borrowed game expression.

## Selection

`gunsmith-icons-direction-b-selected.png`

- cleanest grid and strongest 24–32 px silhouette separation;
- clearest distinction between dimensional Parts, cyan hollow slots, and flat
  octagonal trait emblems;
- best differentiation of standard/long/piercing barrels and ordinary versus
  mastered fire cores;
- coherent cream enamel, dark steel, teal instrumentation, and copper fastener
  language with the selected Equipment family.

## Production boundary

The selected board is a construction reference. Final runtime pixels must be
rebuilt into a committed editable source, exported deterministically into one
named-frame atlas, validated against the exact Part/slot/trait stable-ID set,
and reviewed in the real Gunsmith surface before replacing borrowed art.
