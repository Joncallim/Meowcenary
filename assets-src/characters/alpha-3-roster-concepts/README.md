# Alpha 3 Mercenary redraw provenance

These untouched concept boards are provenance/reference inputs only. Runtime
pixels are the deliberately reconstructed 48x48 sheets under each character's
editable Pixelorama source and deterministic builder.

## Direction B — selected

- File: `direction-b-selected.png`
- SHA-256: `25cdc618848f853e8053d430e3ef0a8e1c26a699245f98e82d6d25e91bbbe729`
- Prompt: "Create an alternative production concept sheet for five ORIGINAL playable Meowcenary characters. Clean pale desaturated workshop-paper background, no words, no labels, no logos, no UI, no weapons. One full-body three-quarter figure per evenly spaced column, same apparent scale. Designed explicitly to reduce to 48x48 game sprites: very bold silhouettes, fewer interior details, large readable accessory shapes, crisp hard-edged limited-palette pixel illustration, dark outline, playful industrial salvage tone without becoming cute mascots. Left to right: (1) Brass Boar: extremely wide low biped boar, wedge snout and two big cream tusks, one dominant dented brass chest shell, short planted legs, oxblood straps, calm. (2) Ember Cougar: athletic upright cougar, low round ears, long dark-tipped tail, charcoal body suit, one broad refractory copper shoulder mantle and three chest vent slits, controlled vermilion heat accents but absolutely no flame aura. (3) Scrap Weasel: unmistakably long stooped weasel with tiny head, huge one-sided salvage satchel, oversized coil disc at belt, hooked sorting tool, olive workwear, quick short legs. (4) Rattle Raptor: compact feathered mechanical raptor, sharp beak profile, extremely long straight balancing tail, digitigrade legs, one magenta monocular optic, bone plates and petrol teal, no rifle and no soldier uniform. (5) Piston Ram: ram automaton with two huge symmetric curved horn arcs, square cobalt/gunmetal chest, big piston forearms, tiny hoof feet, one clear pressure gauge, expressive eyes. Make every silhouette unmistakably different even as flat black shapes. Avoid realistic military insignia, skulls, crowns, medical crosses, pseudo-text, watermarks, and resemblance to existing protected characters. Direction B: graphic arcade-pixel maquette, simplified geometric construction, cooler shadows and brighter identity accents, less texture than painted concept art."
- Selection rationale: the broad plated Boar, restrained vented Cougar,
  asymmetric satchel Weasel, straight-tailed optic Raptor and horn/gauge Ram
  remain separable without labels. The shapes translate cleanly to 48px actors.

## Direction A — rejected

- File: `direction-a-rejected.png`
- SHA-256: `5aad418b73bbefadb101d5474b8d36824ba57a0c00548cf08d33a0292200ac25`
- Prompt: "Create a production concept sheet for five ORIGINAL playable characters in the established Meowcenary visual identity: chunky readable indie pixel-action-game designs, hand-built scrapyard materials, expressive but not cute mascot art, clean neutral warm-gray background, no words, no labels, no logos, no weapons, no UI. One full-body three-quarter character per evenly spaced column, all at the same apparent scale, strong black silhouette readability, limited coherent palette, crisp hard-edged pixel-art concept rendering with enough detail for later reduction to 48x48 sprites. Left to right: (1) BRASS BOAR, a wide low biped boar tank with wedge snout, two unmistakable cream tusks, broad dented aged-brass chest and shoulder plating, short planted legs, thick forearms, oxblood straps, calm unimpressed expression. (2) EMBER COUGAR, sleek muscular upright cougar striker, low rounded ears, long dark-tipped tail, broad shoulders, refractory shoulder mantle and vented copper chest piece, charcoal and burnt vermilion palette, controlled heat with no flame aura. (3) SCRAP WEASEL, long narrow stooped upright weasel engineer, elongated torso, small head, oversized asymmetric salvage satchel, belt magnetic coil, hooked sorting tool, quick short legs, olive workwear and rust hardware. (4) RATTLE RAPTOR, small feathered-mechanical raptor hunter, pronounced beak/snout, long straight stabilizing tail, digitigrade legs, compact monocular optic, light harness, bone feather plates and petrol teal, not a bird soldier. (5) PISTON RAM, horned ram/automaton hybrid with two broad curved horn arcs, box mechanical chest, readable piston forearms, compact hoof feet and small pressure gauge, gunmetal and cobalt with expressive eyes. Keep anatomy and silhouette radically distinct. Avoid military insignia, realistic uniforms, skulls, crowns, protected medical symbols, pseudo-text, watermarks, and resemblance to existing game characters. Direction A: rugged workshop storybook pixel art, slightly chunky proportions, warm materials, strong shape rhythm."
- Rejection rationale: attractive enlarged, but the Cougar and Weasel share a
  similar upright read, the Raptor has more ornamental noise, and dense armour
  would collapse sooner at the shipped actor scale.

Both boards were inspected at native and reduced size. Neither board was
copied directly into runtime assets; the checked-in builders preserve only the
selected shape/material decisions under the established animation and anchor
contract.
